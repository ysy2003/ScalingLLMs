Banana Oatmeal Chocolate Chip Muffins

Easy to make grab 'n go banana oatmeal muffins. It's oatmeal in muffin form!

Course
Breakfast
Cuisine
American
Prep Time
10 minutes
Cook Time
20 minutes
Total Time
30 minutes
Servings
12-14
Calories
200 kcal
Author
Linda Warren

Ingredients

  • 3 cups old fashioned oats
  • 3 Tablespoons brown sugar
  • ½ teaspoon cinnamon
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • 2 cups milk (any kind)
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 medium ripe bananas, mashed
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • ½ cup chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Spray a 12-cup muffin pan with non-stick spray or use paper liners.
  2. In a large bowl, combine the dry ingredients: oats, brown sugar, cinnamon, and salt.
  3. In a separate medium bowl, mash the bananas. Whisk in the milk, eggs, and vanilla extract until well combined.
  4. Pour the wet banana mixture into the dry ingredients. Stir until just combined, being careful not to overmix.
  5. Gently fold in the chocolate chips.
  6. Divide the batter evenly among the 12 muffin cups.
  7. Bake for 20-25 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  8. Let the muffins cool in the pan for a few minutes before transferring them to a wire rack to cool completely.

Notes

Storage & Reheating

To Store: Allow the muffins to cool completely. Place them in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days or in the refrigerator for up to 1 week.

To Freeze: Once completely cool, wrap muffins individually in plastic wrap and place them in a freezer-safe bag or container. They can be frozen for up to 3 months.

To Reheat: Microwave a single muffin for about 30 seconds if thawed or 1 minute if frozen, or until heated through. You can also warm them in a toaster oven.
