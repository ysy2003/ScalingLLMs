Easy M&Ms Easter Individual Parfaits

A delicious and easy-to-make Easter dessert that's perfect for spring celebrations.

Ingredients

  • Whipped Chocolate Ganache layer:
    • ½ cup chocolate chips
    • ¼ cup heavy whipping cream
  • Whipped cream layer:
    • ½ cup heavy whipping cream
    • 2 tablespoons powdered sugar
  • Layers:
    • Graham Cracker Crumbs
    • M&M's Speckled Eggs

Instructions

  1. In a large microwave-proof bowl, heat the chocolate chips and the ¼ cup cream in 30-second increments, stirring well after each, until melted and smooth. Set aside to cool to room temperature. You can place it in the fridge, but don't let it harden.
  2. In a large bowl or the bowl of a stand mixer, add ½ cup whipping cream and the powdered sugar. Whip using a hand-held or stand mixer until stiff peaks form.
  3. Again, using the mixer, whip the cooled chocolate ganache until soft peaks form.
  4. Now, the layering happens! Alternate layers of ganache, whipped cream, graham cracker crumbs, and M&M's in your serving glasses.
