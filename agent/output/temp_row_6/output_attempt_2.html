Case Study: McMahon Engineering

Project Summary

To expand and develop an existing engineering business through the purchase of specialist equipment, enabling the manufacture of various aluminium products for a growing market.

Background

McMahon Engineering is a successful engineering works which has been established for 25 years. The business is located in a large, purpose-built shed beside the promoter's home.

Activities

Funded under Axis 3, through SOAR, the promoter received funding towards an aluminium welder and an aluminium shaper. This new equipment is critical for diversifying their production capabilities.

Benefits

The promoter had enquiries from manufacturers who specialise in the production of aluminium items. The new equipment will enable the promoter to meet the demands of these new customers. One full-time job has also been created as a direct result of this expansion.
