QUALITY

Our goal is to supply quality products at the correct time and at reasonable price. In order to be able to achieve this goal in a systematic way we were certified by the ISO 9001:2000 standard in the year 2000. In the year 2008 we have successfully certified the production of semi-tubular rivets and nails as well logistics services for OEM automotive sector (just-in-time deliveries). In September 2009 all areas were successfully recertified.
