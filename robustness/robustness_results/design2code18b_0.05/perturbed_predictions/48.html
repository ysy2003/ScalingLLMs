BALTIC - Photo: Frank Pichardo Collection

Built by Harlan & Wolf Limited, Belfast, Northern Ireland, 1904. New York service. Largest ship afloat, 1904-05. Troopship during World War I. Scrapped in 1933.

Source: The Statue of Liberty- Ellis Island Foundation. American Family Immigration History Center
