News and Events

McMahon Engineering

Project Summary

To expand and develop an existing engineering business through the purchase of specialist equipment thus enabling the manufacture of various aluminium products.

Background

McMahon Engineering is a successful engineering works which has been established for 25 years. The business is located in a large purpose built shed beside the promoter's home.

Activities

Funded under Axis 3, through SOAR, the promoter received funding towards an aluminium welder and aluminium shaper.

Benefits

The promoter had enquiries from manufacturers who specialise in the production of aluminium items and the equipment will enable the promoter to meet the demands of these new customers.
