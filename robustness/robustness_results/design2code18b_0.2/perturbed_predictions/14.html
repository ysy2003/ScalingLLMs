Photography

Thirty years I have been making drawings, paintings, films and videos. My films integrate hybrid forms such as animation including autobiography, experimental narrative, scripted narrative, ethnographic observation and dance and performance, engaging with all and political themes, such as identity, nationalism, transgression of borders, displacement, nuclear arms, war, foregrounding the human experience and nature.

I am currently working on a series of short films that explore the relationship between landscape and human experience in the context of climate change.
