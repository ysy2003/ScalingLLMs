Highland Basket Hilt Broadword

This basket hilt is produced in 4mm thick mild steel, with typical panel styling and patterns of the period.

The grip can be made in leather or wood with a wire binding, as illustrated, and it has a fullered blade which would have been used for slashing and cutting.

The red wooden liner is an optional extra also.

The blade on this sword is around 32 inches in length and triple fullered.
