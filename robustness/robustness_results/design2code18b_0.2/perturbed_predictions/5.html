PILLARS

EATE H2O program includes five pillars offering in-depth training.

Each of these pillars, there are many options from which students may choose. For each student accepted into the program, the pillar requirements are tailored to the student's educational background, degree course requirements, and area of interest within the First Nations water and sanitation security.

Students will be assigned two professors who will act as mentors. Along with the CREATE H2O program, these professors will assist students in designing the pillar structure and activities that best match their interests and goals.

Students will receive financial support consisting of a stipend and travel awards.

BEGINNING YOUR PROGRAM TO OBTAIN A CO-CURRICULAR RECORD IN FIRST NATIONS WATER AND SANITATION SECURITY

Students must choose between a relevant research-based project (Pillar 1) and a professional work term (Pillar 2), undergraduate students are required to complete 8 portfolio units across at least two of the three pillars before you graduate.
