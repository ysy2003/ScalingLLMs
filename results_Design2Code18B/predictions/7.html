About me

I joined IIT Delhi, I spent an year at Instabase and another couple years as the CTO of Mobile Enerlytics, a startup I co-founded.

I received my Ph.D. from Purdue University where I worked closely with professors Y Charlie Hu and Samuel Middliff . I earned my undergraduate degree from IIT Kanpur .
