Welcome to GlycoFragwork

GlycoFragwork analyzes multiple pre-aligned LC-MS/MS datasets, and reports a list (termined as a 'glycomap') of identified intact glycopeptides with their mass, elution time and abundance

Currently, it can be executed on Windows XP, Vista, 7 with .net framework 2.0 installed.
