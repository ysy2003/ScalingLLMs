The toxicity of ammonia to the marine organisms, sheepshead minnow (Cyprinodon variegatus), mysid (Mysidopsis bahia), and grass shrimp (Palaemonetes pugio)

The discharge of wastewaters containing ammonia has become a major environmental concern, especially in the Chesapeake Bay watershed. A tentative instream limit of 1-2 mg/L for total ammonia has been proposed in Virginia to protect marine aquatic life.
